# About Me

I investigate the gap between how systems claim to work and how they actually behave by building things that make that gap visible.

My background is in fraud detection, Trust & Safety, and personal data protection. For eight years I've been the person who maps where policy breaks down in practice, documents what's actually happening, and translates that into guidance teams can use. I'm currently doing this as a Senior Privacy Advisor at DeleteMe, where I conduct OSINT investigations into data broker compliance.

The projects here are how I am building technical depth to go alongside that work: a portable surveillance awareness device (CBS Scanner) that detects smart glasses, captures WiFi probe requests, and alerts on ALPR camera proximity; a network security monitoring lab running Zeek, Loki, and Grafana; and a privacy-first home network built on Pi-hole and IoT segmentation.

I write publicly about AI governance at digitalnewbie.substack.com, focused on accountability failures and what meaningful oversight of frontier AI actually looks like.
